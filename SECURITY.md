# Security

To report a vulnerability in AltC, the `altc` server and CLI, the Android app or
AltC Cloud, write to altc@alterndigital.com with a subject that starts
`security:`. Please don't open a public issue.

What's in scope, how quickly we reply, and our safe harbour for good-faith
research are on [altc.alterndigital.com/security](https://altc.alterndigital.com/security).
How AltC protects your computer is in the
[security model](https://altc.alterndigital.com/security-model).
